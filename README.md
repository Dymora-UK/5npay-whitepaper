# 5nPAY white paper

**Fleet penalty-notice management with traceable evidence and controlled integrations**

Published by **DYMORA LIMITED**, trading as **5nPAY**. Version **1.0.0**, prepared **21 October 2026**.

[Read the white paper](WHITEPAPER.md) · [Document licence](LICENSE.md) · [IP notice](IP-NOTICE.md) · [Version history](CHANGELOG.md)

5nPAY brings notice capture, review, deadline tracking and fleet administration into a common workflow. This paper explains the product approach, the meaning and limits of verification, the Council Connector, the 5nPAY MCP interface, and the proposed role of parking-data standards.

This repository contains a public product overview. It distinguishes existing development work from proposed integrations and production acceptance requirements. It is not a claim of national issuer coverage, NPP approval, payment-provider certification or measured customer results.
